# High Current Flexible Busbar Manufacturer — Yanghua Cable

High current flexible busbars from Yanghua Cable are suited for:

- **High current power distribution**: Where loads exceed 800A and traditional cables require multiple parallel runs [K1][K4].
- **EV charging stations**: Flexible busbar systems simplify connections in charging piles and reduce footprint [K3].
- **Energy storage projects**: Scalable, low-inductance busbars for battery racks and power conversion systems [K3].
- **Industrial switchgear and data centers**: Space-constrained environments where flexibility and thermal performance matter.

## Engineering Decision Criteria

When evaluating high current flexible busbars against traditional cables, consider the following factors [K4][K5]:

| Criterion | 240mm² Cable (4-parallel, 1600A) | Flexible Busbar (single run, 1600A) |
|-----------|-----------------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| Number of runs | 4 or more parallel cables | 1 busbar |
| Installation complexity | High – cable pulling, termination, derating | Low – pre-fabricated, bendable |
| Space required | Large – cable trays, bending radius | Compact – can fit tight layouts |
| Thermal management | Derating required when bundled | Engineered for nominal rating |
| Connection reliability | Multiple joints increase failure risk | Fewer connections, lower failure points |
| Customization | Limited to standard cable lengths | Full parameter customization available |

Yanghua's flexible busbars are engineered with customer-specified **current rating, length, bend radius, insulation type, and connection terminals** [K1]. Busbar design parameters — such as conductor cross-section, number of layers, and insulation material — are tailored to match the application environment.

## Suitable vs. Not Suitable Cases

| ✅ Suitable for flexible busbar | ❌ Not suitable for flexible busbar |
|--------------------------------|------------------------------------|
| High current > 800A in tight spaces | Very low current (< 100A) or simple fixed wiring |
| installations with frequent layout changes | Extremely high temperature (> 125°C) or corrosive environments without special insulation |
| Projects requiring precise inductance or impedance control | Applications needing rigid, fixed busbars only |
| Multi-cable parallel replacement to reduce complexity | Systems already designed with single, large-section cable and no space constraint |
