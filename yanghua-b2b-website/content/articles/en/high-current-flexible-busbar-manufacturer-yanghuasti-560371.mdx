# High Current Flexible Busbar Manufacturer – Yanghua Cable

When you need a custom high current flexible busbar for power distribution, Yanghua Cable (Yanghuasti) supplies UL-recognized, single-insulated flexible busbars rated up to 3000 A, in widths from 20 to 300 mm, with lengths up to 3000 mm [K1]. These busbars are cut to size, drilled, and tinned for bolted connections, making them a direct alternative to traditional copper cables in heavy electrical systems.

## Application Scenario

Yanghua flexible busbars serve as a compact, high-current busbar solution in enclosed power distribution equipment—including switchgear, panel boards, busways, and control cabinets [K1]. Their thin, flat profile (typically 0.3–1.0 mm per layer) allows for efficient current flow and heat dissipation in constrained spaces. Typical voltage ratings cover 600 V to 1000 V AC/DC, depending on insulation [K1].

**Key engineering judgments from source [K1]:**
- Flat conductor geometry reduces skin effect at high frequencies.
- Laminated structure provides mechanical flexibility.
- Copper foil layers are available in multiple thicknesses.

## Suitable / Not Suitable Cases

![image](/storage/uploads/images/2026/07/seedream-li1-07.jpg)

**Suitable:**
- High-density power distribution in switchgear panels where space is tight.
- Battery-to-inverter connections in energy storage systems (single insulated).
- Bus bar replacement for copper cables in retrofit upgrades.

**Not suitable:**
- Outdoor or moisture-exposed applications (single insulation is not weatherproof; specify double insulation).
- Applications requiring direct burial or contact with corrosive chemicals.
- Continuous bending or dynamic flexing (busbar is for static installation only).

## Procurement Notes

To proceed with ordering a custom flexible busbar from Yanghua:
1. Specify current rating (A), width (mm), length (mm), and hole pattern.
2. Confirm insulation type: single wrap for indoor, double wrap for higher isolation [K1].
3. Request tinning of terminal ends for corrosion resistance and low contact resistance.
4. Lead times vary by custom work; contact Yanghua for current production schedule.

## FAQ

**Q: Can these busbars carry 3000 A continuously in a 40°C ambient?**  
A: The source material states maximum 3000 A rating, but de-rating applies based on enclosure ventilation, conductor cross-section, and allowable temperature rise. Consult Yanghua for your specific enclosure thermal model.

**Q: What is the minimum bending radius for a 100 mm wide busbar?**  
A: Flexible busbars can be formed manually; however, repeated bending may crack insulation. Yanghua recommends custom pre-formed shapes for production orders.

**Q: Are UL or CE certifications available?**  
A: Yanghua lists UL recognition on the material components; confirm current certifications for your region with the sales team before ordering.

**Q: Do you supply double-insulated versions for safety-critical systems?**  
A: Yes, double-insulation options are available—verify with Yanghua for the specific insulation grade and dielectric strength.

**Q: How is the busbar terminated for battery lugs?**  
A: Tin-plated copper ends with pre-drilled holes to match standard terminal bolts. Provide a hole pattern diagram with your order.
