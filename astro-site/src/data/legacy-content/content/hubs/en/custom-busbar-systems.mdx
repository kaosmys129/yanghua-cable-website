## Tailored solutions

Custom flexible busbar systems are specified around the installation rather than around a generic product name. The design brief should describe the current, voltage, available space, route geometry, enclosure interface, environmental exposure, and the termination method required at each end. This information lets the engineering team decide whether a flexible busbar is suitable and which accessories are needed.

## What to confirm before requesting a design

- **Electrical duty:** operating current, system voltage, fault level, duty cycle, and the required conductor material.
- **Mechanical route:** overall length, bend direction, minimum bend radius, support points, and the distance between equipment terminals.
- **Environment:** indoor or outdoor installation, water or dust exposure, ambient temperature, chemicals, vibration, and cleaning method.
- **Interfaces:** switchgear, transformer, inverter, battery cabinet, or motor connection details, including terminal dimensions and access constraints.
- **Verification:** applicable standards, insulation requirements, temperature-rise data, short-circuit withstand, drawings, and inspection records.

A customized assembly should not be selected only by matching an ampere number. The final design must be checked against the actual installation temperature, enclosure ventilation, connection hardware, and local electrical requirements. Where the project requires fire-rated circuits, traction power, or a higher fault level than the product documentation supports, a different distribution method may be more appropriate.
