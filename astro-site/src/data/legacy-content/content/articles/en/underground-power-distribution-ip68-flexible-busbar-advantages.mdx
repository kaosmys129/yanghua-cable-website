# Underground Power Distribution: IP68 Flexible Busbar Advantages

When you need a flexible, fully waterproof power distribution solution for underground tunnels, wet environments, or outdoor cable transitions, the IP68 flexible busbar system offers a proven alternative to traditional cables or rigid busbar trunks. It provides continuous sealing against water ingress (submersion up to 1 meter for 30 minutes), high mechanical flexibility for routing in confined spaces, and simplified jointing compared to cable-based installations [K1]. This makes it particularly suitable for applications where water resistance, space constraints, and ease of installation are critical engineering priorities.

## Application Scenarios

The IP68 flexible busbar is engineered for power distribution in environments where water, dust, or physical stress are constant factors. Typical scenarios include:

- **Underground cable tunnels and subways** – where condensation, flooding risk, and limited access demand a sealed, corrosion-resistant system.
- **Outdoor cable transitions and trenches** – connecting power from ground-level switchgear to underground distribution points.
- **Industrial wet areas** – such as water treatment plants, food processing lines, or wash-down zones requiring IP68 ingress protection.
- **Temporary power installations** – where flexible routing and rapid deployment are needed, yet the system must withstand rain or ground moisture.

## Engineering Decision Criteria

When evaluating the IP68 flexible busbar against traditional cable or rigid busbar, consider these key parameters:

| Parameter | IP68 Flexible Busbar | Traditional PVC/XLPE Cables | Rigid Busbar Trunking |
|-----------|----------------------|----------------------------|-----------------------|
| **Water protection** | IP68 (submersible) [K1] | Lower IP rating typical (IP44–IP67) | IP54–IP68 possible, but joints critical |
| **Flexibility** | High – can bend to fit obstacles | Moderate – requires minimum bend radius | Low – straight runs only |
| **Number of joints per run** | Minimal – often joint-less per coil | Many – each drum needs joint | Many – at each section |
| **Installation time** | Fast – no cable glanding or complex sealing | Moderate – cable pulling & termination | Slow – assembly of sections |
| **Continuous rating** | Up to 630A (example range) | Up to 630A typical | Up to 5000A possible |

Choose the IP68 flexible busbar when:

- The route involves multiple bends, tight spaces, or existing obstacles.
- Water exposure is frequent or unpredictable; a fully submersible rating is mandatory.
- Installation speed and reduced labour cost are priorities.
- You want to eliminate weak points at cable joints or termination boxes.

## Suitable vs. Not Suitable Cases

| Suitable ✅ | Not Suitable ❌ |
|-------------|-----------------|
| Underground tunnel distribution up to 630A | Very high current applications above 630A |
| Lighting or small power distribution in damp environments | Feeder circuits for large motors or transformers |
| Temporary site power with weather exposure | Long straight runs where rigid busbar is cheaper |
| Retrofit in existing cable trenches | Applications requiring fire-rated or fire-resistant busbar (no fireproof claims in source material) |
| Connections requiring multi-directional routing | High-voltage primary distribution above 1 kV |

**Note:** The source material does not include fire resistance certification or specific fireproof test reports. For fire-rated applications, verify with the manufacturer whether alternative products or additional protection are needed.

## Procurement Notes

Before specifying the IP68 flexible busbar system, consider:

![image](/storage/uploads/images/2026/07/seedream-li2-09.jpg)

- **Rating confirmation:** Ensure the amperage (e.g., up to 630A) and voltage class match your load requirements [K1].
- **Installation accessories:** Request a full list of compatible sealing kits, support hangers, and termination enclosures.
- **Length per coil:** Understand maximum continuous length available without joints to minimise weak points.
- **Thermal rating:** Check continuous operating temperature range and de-rating factors if installed in high-ambient tunnels.
- **Testing evidence:** Ask the supplier for IP68 test reports – genuine verification under IEC 60529 for submersion conditions.
- **Lead time:** Factory-flexible busbar may have shorter lead times than rigid sections, but confirm with supplier.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: Can the IP68 flexible busbar be bent on-site without special tools?**
Yes, the busbar is designed for manual flexible bending during installation to fit around obstacles, making it easier than cable bending or rigid busbar sections [K1].

**Q2: How does the IP68 rating prevent water ingress over time?**
The busbar body and end seals are manufactured as a continuous, extruded system with no openings along the run. Properly installed termination enclosures maintain the IP68 integrity. Regular inspection of end seals is recommended.

**Q3: Is the flexible busbar suitable for both AC and DC applications?**
The source material supports AC distribution up to 630A. For DC applications, verify with the manufacturer regarding voltage drop, corona effects, and compatible switchgear.

**Q4: What is the typical lifespan of an IP68 flexible busbar in underground conditions?**
Product lifespan depends on installation quality, water chemistry, and mechanical stress. The source material does not specify a warranty period, so request specific manufacturer data on long-term performance in your environment.

**Q5: Can the busbar be reused or relocated after initial installation?**
Flexible busbar can be carefully removed and re-installed if undamaged. However, each re-installation requires new sealing kits and termination accessories to maintain the IP68 rating. Budget for replacement of sealing components.
