## Direct Answer

Flexible busbar delivers a compact, high-current power distribution solution for modern data centers, supporting 400A to 6400A with IP68 protection and rapid installation.

## Why Data Centers Adopt Flexible Busbar


| Factor           | Traditional Cable   | Flexible Busbar            |
| ---------------- | ------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Install Time     | 3–5 days per floor | 1–2 days per floor        |
| Space Needed     | Cable tray stacks   | Flat, bendable busbar      |
| Heat Dissipation | Limited by bundling | Excellent laminated design |
| Maintenance      | Difficult access    | Modular, easy swap         |

## Suitable Cases

- **Tier III / IV data centers** — redundant power paths with high reliability
- **Hyperscale facilities** — fast deployment at scale
- **Edge data centers** — constrained physical space
- **Colocation facilities** — frequent power reconfiguration

## Installation Checklist

1. Match busbar current rating to UPS/PDU output
2. Maintain bend radius ≥ 2× busbar thickness
3. Use manufacturer-supplied mounting brackets
4. Test insulation resistance before energization
